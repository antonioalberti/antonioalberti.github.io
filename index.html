About Me

Biography

I am an Associate Professor in Software Engineering at the School of Computer Science, University of Leeds, UK. I have over 20 years of experience in telecommunications and computer engineering, with a research focus on next-generation information architectures, 5G/6G technologies, and future Internet alternatives.

My research explores design, softwarization, virtualization, self-organization, orchestration, and programmability of converging information and communication technologies. I am particularly involved in advanced network architectures, smart city development, and digital monetization approaches.

I served as the Chief Architect of the NovaGenesis Future Internet project (2008–2024) and contributed to the architecture of the Brazil 6G Project. I have also contributed to Future Internet initiatives in South Korea and participated in the initial discussions of the Brazilian National IoT Plan.

I am the author of "Novos Renascimentos," a book covering hundreds of technologies and their impacts on society. Since 2018, I have dedicated myself to futurism, helping institutions position themselves towards futures of human values supported by technology.

Current Research

My current research focuses on agentic AI and its applications in future internet architectures, software engineering, and communication systems.

(i)

Multi-Agentic-AI Protocols

Design and implementation of communication protocols for coordinating multiple AI agents in distributed environments.

(ii)

Secure Sandboxed AI Agents and Data Management

Developing secure isolation mechanisms and data governance frameworks for safe AI agent deployment.

(iii)

Monetised 6G Architecture

Exploring digital monetization models and economic frameworks for next-generation 6G networks and services.

(iv)

New Link Layer Protocol Alternative to Ethernet/Wi-Fi

Research on novel link layer protocols that can overcome limitations of current Ethernet and Wi-Fi technologies.

(v)

Improvement of NovaGenesis Architecture Using Agentic AI

Enhancing the NovaGenesis Future Internet architecture with modern agentic AI capabilities for autonomous network operation.
